# impl-165 - A crossing must wait for the cell it lands in (audit finding 1)

Owned files: crates/engine/src/transition.rs

Acceptance: cargo test -p engine --lib,cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings,cargo fmt --all -- --check

Track: portal.

## The finding

`destination_is_resident()` in `transition.rs` takes `destination_keys(...).first()`. For an
exterior destination the keys are generated by looping from `-DOOR_PRESTREAM_GRID_RADIUS` (= 1)
upward, so the first key is the **south-west neighbour** of the landing cell, not the landing cell.
A crossing can therefore be accepted before its real destination cell is resident, or held forever
because an unrelated neighbour is missing or failed. (A second reader confirmed the key order in the
source.)

Suggested fix: readiness is judged on the landing cell itself, built directly from
`destination_grid()`, and the prestream list stays as it is. Test both combinations - neighbour
resident but landing cell still loading (must not be ready), landing resident but a neighbour
missing or failed (must be ready) - with and without a `DoorAnchor`. Interiors are unaffected
(one key); keep a test that says so.
