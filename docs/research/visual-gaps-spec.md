Merged report of DeepSeek lead-026 and its research and websearch workers, 2026-09-22/23. Kept verbatim as the spec for impl-036..039; see the per-gap verification notes inside.


**Scope note up front, because it changes how to read the evidence.** My session and every worker I spawned had **Read/Grep/Glob only — no shell**. The harness denies `python`, `cargo` and `git` outright (it told two of my workers so explicitly: *"inline python -c is always denied for workers; give it a script file"*). The project allowlist in `.deepseek-agents.json:21` does contain `Bash(python tools/*)`, but that is not what spawn_workers' read-only workers receive. So **no worker could dump a record field or a database row**, and neither could I. The brief's "at least one data dump per gap" is therefore met only partly, and I say per gap exactly what was and was not verified. Everything below that is marked *verified* was checked by me directly (a dump I ran, a file I read, or a `file:line` I opened); anything else is attributed to the worker that claimed it and often to a documentation source.

A second, systematic caveat: the serialised `records.data` / `cells.data` **are** in the shipped database (I verified `XCLL`, `WTHR` and `LGTM` tags are present in `skyrim_world.db`), so no gap needs a re-read of the ESMs — but their *values* have never been read by anything in this project, and the record-layout knowledge below for `XCLL`/`WTHR`/`LGTM` comes from web documentation, not from a byte of our own data.

## Gap 1 — Glow / emissive

**What Skyrim does.** Emissive is `BSLightingShaderProperty`'s emissive colour × emissive multiple, with the glow mask in texture slot 2 (`_g.dds`), read only when the shader type is `Glow` or `SLSF1_Own_Emit`/`SLSF2_Glow_Map` (bit 6) is set. `BSEffectShaderProperty` cards (torches, the Dwemer glow fills) take the colour from base colour and the magnitude from `base_color_scale`, and carry no separate glow mask. Numbers from the converted glbs: `blackreachsun01` material `BlackreachSun01:20` has `emissiveFactor [1.0, 0.851, 0.247]`, `emissiveStrength 3.0`, no map (the whole shape is the orb); `blackreachgiantmushroom01` is emissive on all five materials — caps `[0.212,0.992,1.0]`×2.0, ×2.0, `[0.627,0.996,1.0]`×3.6, `[0.765,0.996,1.0]`×2.0 with glow maps `blackreachmushroom01_g`/`02_g`/`glowingmushrooms01_g`, plus an additive card ×1.21; `blackreachlargedripanim01` `:12` ×2.0 with a map, `:11` an additive card ×1.21; `fxglowfillrndlitmid` factor `[1,1,1]`, no strength, additive. 149 `_g.ktx2` are published. `.bgem`/`.bgsm` material files are never opened by the converter (grep: zero hits) and whether any Blackreach model depends on one is undetermined.

**What we do today.** Nothing is broken in the pipeline. Converter: `material.rs:594-625` writes `emissiveFactor`, `emissiveTexture` and `KHR_materials_emissive_strength`; slot classification at `:1028-1043`; effect path at `:980-981`. Bevy 0.19 **does** honour the extension: `bevy_gltf-0.19.0/src/loader/mod.rs:1415` — `base_emissive * material.emissive_strength().unwrap_or(1.0)` **[verified by me]**. The engine preserves it: `render.rs:717-762` republishes `StandardMaterial { alpha_mode, ..material }` **[verified by me]**; `streaming.rs:1831-1841` only validates. The gap is a **unit mismatch**: emissive sits at raw glTF magnitude 1.21–3.6 while the ambient floor is 800 interior / 650 cavern (`app.rs:1358`, `:1377`) **[verified by me]** and light pools are ≈50× the ambient (`lights.rs:91`). Nothing rescales emissive; no `Bloom`, no `Hdr` anywhere in the engine (grep; the only `Tonemapping` is `None` on the portal camera, `portal.rs:734`) **[verified by me]**.

**Spec — engine only, no schema, no reconversion.**
- `crates/engine/src/lights.rs`: add `EMISSIVE_EXPOSURE: f32 ≈ 1000.0` beside `LIGHT_EXPOSURE` (`:91`); order of magnitude, to be fitted.
- `crates/engine/src/render.rs:717-762`: scale in the existing handler, but **restructure the early return at `:732-734`** so the handler runs for materials *without* an `OPEN_SKYRIM_material` blend pair — the mushroom caps are `OPAQUE`/`MASK` and are missed today. Rename to `SkyrimMaterialHandler`, update the doc comment at `:709-715`.
- `crates/engine/src/app.rs`: `Hdr` + `Bloom::NATURAL` on the main camera (both available; `bevy` is a default-features workspace dependency, `Cargo.toml:12`).
- Tests: handler scales emissive with and without a blend pair; strengthen `app.rs:623-624` so "emissive intensity was lost" also asserts the emissive is within a factor of the ambient.
- Verify: **render `SR-place-Blackreach_04` — it has never been rendered** (`local/poses2/final-4x3/` holds `_01`..`_03` only) **[verified by me]**. Metric: clipped %/near-90 % on the orb and mushroom frames, plus `exposure_stats.py`; fit `EMISSIVE_EXPOSURE` on the tuning set and confirm on `local/calib/holdout_shots.json`.

**Effort/risk.** Half a day plus a tuning pass. Risk: every emissive surface in Tamriel brightens too, and the exterior pool was signed off at 0.97× — the Phase 2 visual review has to be redone. Bloom interacts with the portal camera's deliberate `Tonemapping::None`.

## Gap 2 — Per-cell lighting and fog

**What Skyrim does** (web documentation, UESP + Mutagen's generated parsers; **not** verified against our blobs):
- `CELL` `XCLL` = 92 bytes (`0x5C`), colours 4 bytes each: `0x00` ambient, `0x04` directional, `0x08` fog colour (near), `0x0C` fog near, `0x10` fog far, `0x14`/`0x18` directional rotation, `0x1C` directional fade, `0x20` fog clip (a *clip plane*, not fog), `0x24` fog power (1 = linear), `0x28-0x3F` six axis ambient tints, `0x40` specular, `0x44` fresnel, `0x48` fog far colour, `0x4C` fog max ("not used"), `0x50`/`0x54` light fade start/end, `0x58` **inherit flags** (11 bits: ambient, directional, fog colour, fog near, fog far, directional rotation, directional fade, clip, fog power, fog max, light fade).
- `LTMP` → `LGTM`: the same 92-byte payload, whose last field is *not* inherit flags, plus `DALC` (32 bytes). **The polarity of the inherit bits is unresolved by every source** — UESP/fopdoc naming says "set = inherited", the CK wiki text says "ticking = override", and its two mirrors contradict each other. It must be settled from data: AlftandZCell is **4.5× too bright** and needs a template several times darker than Alftand02's, so the reading that produces that is the right one (three records, arithmetic, no GPU).
- `WTHR`: subrecord stream — `*0TX` cloud textures, `LNAM` layer count, `RNAM`/`QNAM` cloud speeds, `PNAM`/`JNAM` cloud colours/alphas, `NAM0` = 17 colour groups × 16 bytes (Sunrise/Day/Sunset/Night) indexed **0 sky-upper, 1 fog near, 3 ambient, 4 sunlight, 5 sun, 7 sky-lower, 8 horizon, 12 fog far**, `FNAM` = 8 fog floats (day/night near/far/power/max), `DATA` scalars + classification flags (`0x01` pleasant, `0x02` cloudy, `0x04` rainy, `0x08` snow), `TNAM` sky statics, `DALC`, `MODL`. `CLMT`: `WLST` 12-byte entries (weather, chance %, `GLOB`), `FNAM` = sun texture, `TNAM` = sunrise/sunset/volatility. `REGN.RDWT` is a region weather list that is community-attested to override the climate.
- Blackreach draws the ordinary weather-driven **sky dome**, not a special backdrop: UESP says the game "considers it to be outdoors"; `BlackreachClimate` and `BlackreachWeather` exist in `Skyrim.esm` **[verified by a worker's grep; I verified the greps are plausible but did not run them myself]**, and `BlackreachClimate` is reported to point its sun/glare textures at `black.dds`.

**What we do today.** The converter reads **none** of it — `XCLL|LTMP|LGTM|WTHR|CLMT|XCCM` have **zero** occurrences in `crates/converter/src` **[verified by me]**, `extract_cell_info` reads `XCLC` + `EDID` only, and the `worldspaces` table stores no `CNAM` climate (`exporter.rs:28-30`) **[verified by me]**. The engine has three hard-coded states: `SKY_COLOR`/`UNDERGROUND_COLOR` (`app.rs:1350-1351`), `INTERIOR_AMBIENT_*` (`:1358`, `:1367`), `CAVERN_AMBIENT_*` (`:1374`, `:1377`), `UNDERGROUND_WORLDSPACES = [0x1EE62, 0x69857]` (`:1378-1380`), all applied by `update_atmosphere` (`:1395-1444`) **[all verified by me]**. Interiors get fog that deliberately cannot reach geometry (`fog_off`, `app.rs:1537`); ambient is a global `GlobalAmbientLight` resource, not a per-camera `AmbientLight`; the clear colour is global. Measured consequences are already in the repo: AlftandZCell 4.5× and `LTMP` named as the reason (`calibration.md:53-59`), Alftand02's median matching while the haze is missing (`:80-87`), Blackreach's backdrop black where every reference is teal.

**Spec.**
- Converter: `parse_xcll` (branch on length: 40/64/72/92 — read the real lengths first), `parse_lgtm` (DATA flags + XCLL), `parse_clmt`, `parse_wthr`; add `LTMP` and `WRLD CNAM` to `is_form_id_subrecord` (`esm/mod.rs:162-175`) or the FormIDs are wrong for every non-`Skyrim.esm` plugin. Publish one flat **`space_lighting`** table in a second pass inside `export_to_db` (space_id, is_interior, template_id, ambient/directional/fog packed INTEGER, fog_near/far/power/clip REAL, direction_rot_xy/z INTEGER, direction_fade REAL, sky_upper/sky_fog/sky_lower INTEGER, sun INTEGER, sun_illuminance REAL, climate_id, weather_id, has_sky INTEGER) — resolve the `LTMP`/`CNAM` chains once at conversion time, deterministically (first `WLST` entry, not a random weather).
- Engine: new `crates/engine/src/world/lighting.rs` with `SpaceLighting` + a `SpaceLightingCatalog` preloaded at startup exactly like `AssetCatalog` preloads `landscape_textures`/`waters` (`world/database.rs:165-193`); `update_atmosphere` looks up one row per `ActiveCell` change and writes per-camera `AmbientLight` (which overrides `GlobalAmbientLight` for its camera — `bevy_light-0.19.0/src/ambient_light.rs:6-30`), `DistanceFog` from the space's colour + near/far, `Camera.clear_color = ClearColorConfig::Custom(...)` (also on the portal camera, so a doorway stops clearing to Alftand's sky), and the sun's colour/illuminance from the weather; `has_sky = 0` replaces the `UNDERGROUND_WORLDSPACES` constant. Snap on a crossing (the real game stops at a load screen), keep the terrain-ring fog's "no cliff" guarantee (`app.rs:1812-1880`) by taking only the colour from the weather, and leave `lights.rs:95-99` pinned so point lights do not move with the room. No change to `streaming.rs` or the streaming payload.
- Schema: **5 → 6** (`shared/src/lib.rs:7`) and a reconversion (or a targeted backfill from `records.data`, whose rkyv layout is known but needs the `pointer_width` feature matched).
- Tests: the parsers on hand-built and recorded slices, an `export_to_db` fixture resolving `LTMP`→`LGTM` and `WRLD`→`CLMT`→`WTHR`, the space-choice as a pure function (mirroring the existing `exterior_fog` tests), a "no interior gets unreachable fog any more" regression, and a holdout pass over `local/calib/holdout_shots.json`.
- Acceptance numbers, already measured: AlftandZCell 4.5× → ≈1×, Alftand02's p50 ≈1.1× must hold while the dark band's hue moves, Blackreach's black-backdrop fraction must collapse, and the exterior's top strip must stop being the (133,163,204) wedge.

**Effort/risk.** ~2.5 days plus the reconversion. Risks, in order: the `XCLL` offsets are unverified (first task = read the lengths and near/far out of the blobs; the failure is loud, not silent), the `LGTM` inherit polarity, the `WTHR` layout needs one real record before any weather code, one shared brightness constant must be re-fitted, and the terrain-ring/weather-fog tension is the likely review round.

## Gap 3 — Directional snow and ice

**What Skyrim does** (documentation + one decisive local dump):
- `STAT` `DNAM` = **8 bytes**: `float Max Angle` (CK: 30–120 degrees, "controls how much of the object is covered") and a `MATO` FormID. `MATO` `DATA` (~48 bytes) carries Falloff Scale, Falloff Bias, Noise UV Scale, Material UV Scale, DirProjVector X/Y/Z, Normal Dampener, Single Pass Color RGB and a Single Pass bool, and **no texture set**. Coverage is a **whole-object projected material** evaluated from the surface normal against the projection vector and the max angle — **there is no per-triangle material type in Skyrim NIFs** — modulated by vertex-colour alpha (documented for LOD snow at ≈0.4 light / ≈0.5 heavy; extrapolation to full-detail statics is unconfirmed). The reference frame and the exact blend formula are undocumented.
- **The snow variants share the plain model — verified by my own dump of the database:** grepping `skyrim_world.db` returned `…Roof01SnowHeavy` immediately followed by `Dungeons\Dwemer\Facades\DweFacadeTowerRoof01.nif`, and there is **no** `*snow*.nif`/`.glb` for any Dwemer facade, while genuinely snow-named models (`SnowDriftM01`, …) *are* converted. So the snow is the `DNAM`→`MATO` projected material, and that is the fix.
- Roof appearance: `dwefacadetowerroof01.glb` has `baseColorFactor [1,1,1]`, base colour `dwemetaltiles01`, normal `dwemetaltiles01_n`, and environment cube `textures/cubemaps/bronze_e.ktx2` (closure entry, **read by me**); the converter hardcodes `metallicFactor 0.0` and `roughnessFactor = 1 - glossiness/100` (`material.rs:513-517`, **verified by me**). So the gold is the base texture, the polish is glossiness, and the missing snow material is the dominant difference.
- Ice: the Alftand02 ice set is `Dungeons\Caves\Ice\**` (`ShaftRock\CaveIShaftR*`, `Cliffs\CaveICliffs*`, `FrozenWall\CaveIFWall*`, `Balcony\CaveIBalcony*`; 508 converted glbs), base colour `icefrozen01`, a normal map, and slot 6 = `icecavesubsurfacetint01` (inner layer). The converter classifies slots 4/5 as `EnvironmentCube`/`EnvironmentMask` and 6 as `InnerLayer` (`material.rs:1016-1062`) and writes them into the `OPEN_SKYRIM_material` extension; the engine **discards them** — there is no `EnvironmentMapLight`, `LightProbe` or `IrradianceVolume` anywhere in `crates/engine/src` **[verified by me]**. Missing environment response is the dominant cause; the worker flagged that it could not sample the base texture's pixels, so a dark albedo may co-contribute.

**Spec.**
- Converter (snow): read `STAT` `DNAM` (`esm/exporter.rs` STAT branch `:195`); add **nullable `statics.material_object INTEGER`** and **`statics.material_max_angle REAL`**, plus a new **`matos`** table (id INTEGER PK, editor_id TEXT, falloff_scale REAL, falloff_bias REAL, noise_uv_scale REAL, material_uv_scale REAL, dir_proj_x/y/z REAL, normal_dampener REAL, single_pass_color INTEGER, single_pass INTEGER). Add `DNAM` to the STAT case in `is_form_id_subrecord` (`esm/mod.rs`).
- Engine (snow): `ExtendedMaterial<StandardMaterial, SnowExtension>` with `crates/engine/src/snow.rs` + `snow.wgsl`, following the existing `TerrainMaterial`/`WaterMaterial` pattern (`render.rs:38-39`); the extension carries `dir_proj`, `cos_max_angle`, falloff/bias, dampener, snow colour; per-reference material assignment keyed on `material_object`.
- Engine (ice): bind the cube map the converter already emits — read `extensions.OPEN_SKYRIM_material.textureSlots` in the material handler (`render.rs:586-700`) and attach an `EnvironmentMapLight`/probe. Larger than the snow half; **recommend splitting it out**.
- Schema: snow needs **5 → 6 and a database rebuild** (no glTF rebuild); ice needs a targeted glTF reconvert **only if** cube maps are re-emitted.
- Verify: `SR-place-Alftand_Ruined_Tower` plus `_02`/`_05`/`_06` for the roofs; `SR-interior-Alftand_04`/`_05`/`_07`/`_08`/`_09` for the ice. Fit the falloff on `DweFacadeTowerRoof01SnowHeavy`, hold out `DweFacadeTower1x3Snow` / `Arch01Snow` / `Tower1x4Snow` at (77992,78336) and (76240,77035).

**Effort/risk.** Snow: converter ~150 lines + schema + 4 tests, engine ~120 lines + small WGSL; medium, and the risk is concentrated in the undocumented `Max Angle` semantics and whether `dir_proj` is object- or world-space. Ice: medium-to-high, larger.

## Gap 4 — Broken and odd objects

**(1) BlackPlane01 and the black voids.** I read `meshes/markers/blackplane01.glb` myself: 4 vertices, 2 triangles, POSITION min/max ±256 (a flat 512×512 quad), `alphaMode OPAQUE`, base colour white × `textures/black.opensky-srgb.ktx2`, `metallicFactor 0.0`, `roughnessFactor 0.2`, `KHR_materials_specular` colour `[0,0,0]`, **not** double-sided. It is a deliberate black backdrop with no alpha trickery, so drawing it black is correct behaviour — the defect is that a still cannot tell it from the *other* black cause: the clear colour `UNDERGROUND_COLOR = srgb(0.015,0.02,0.035)` ≈ rgb(4,5,9) (`app.rs:1351`, `:1416-1420`) seen wherever geometry is not resident, and in Blackreach/AlftandWorld the resident grid is only 20,480 units across (`stream_radius = 2`, 4096-unit cells) while the caverns are far wider. The worker also ruled out the portal for the corridor-door frame: the shot poses are 1157 and 1457 units from door `00092809`, beyond `DOOR_PRESTREAM_RADIUS = 800` (`transition.rs:27`), so the leaf must be drawn closed and that black is the same hole class. **Fix: run the discriminator first** — a throwaway engine override that paints the `BlackPlane01` family magenta in `streaming::spawn_cell`, re-render, and look: magenta ⇒ the planes; still black ⇒ the clear colour, in which case the fix is gap 2's fog/backdrop and not a geometry hunt. Do not hide `BlackPlane01`.

**(2) The two Blackreach models that fail validation.** The error is real and gate-breaking: `"converted model has no validated aggregate bounds; reconvert the asset"` at `streaming.rs:1639-1641` (**verified by me** — worker 4 cited 1633-1635, which is the function's start) feeding `transform_bounds_validation_failures`, a hard requirement in `app.rs:1757` and `metrics.rs:351`. I read `meshes/furniture/creatureexit/wispambush.glb`: `{"scenes":[{"name":"wispambush","nodes":[]}]}` — an **empty scene**, and the converter builds it on purpose (drop `EditorMarker` geometry then `empty_scene_glb`, `mesh.rs:89-104`, **verified by me**). `integration.rs:102-117` then fails `glb_bounds` and leaves `bounds_valid` at its `DEFAULT 0` (**verified by me**). So two invisible markers in cell `0002D4E0` block a measured Blackreach run. Fix: converter-owned — a typed `GlbBoundsError::Empty` and a **`statics.model_empty INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0`** written on that cause only (keeping `unbounded_model_count` for everything else), with the engine skipping asset spawn for such references and counting them separately; or, as an unblocker needing no reconversion, an engine-only reorder that accepts a spawned scene with no render primitives while keeping every other failure fatal. Prefer the flag; take the engine-only change if the reconversion window slips (research-031 owns the disk budget).

**(3) The 4334-radius light.** Numbers verified from `local/calib/calibration.md:61-72` (whose source was a real `space_lights.py` run): `000D9051` / `FalmerCityLight02NS` at (2122,9014,4668), colour (216,128,39), radius 4334, falloff 1.0, 71× a 512 torch, and `416/504` records are falloff 1.0 so the exponent is not the cause. The engine fit `intensity_for_radius` (`lights.rs:156-159`, **verified by me**: `HALF_RADIUS_ILLUMINANCE * (radius/2)² / window`) is the cause; Bevy 0.19 has `range` with a hard inverse-square window and **no** `decay`/`decay_falloff`. Documentation says Skyrim's brightness scale is `FNAM` **fade** (torch 1.35), the radius is reach with a "crude" non-inverse-square falloff, and physical inverse-square is opt-in per record; portal-strict culling is an interior-room mechanism, so it cannot explain a worldspace light. Fix: add `INTENSITY_REFERENCE_RADIUS = 256.0` and use `(radius * 0.5).min(that)` in `intensity_for_radius` — **byte-identical for every radius ≤ 512**, so the calibrated torches cannot move, and the FalmerCity light drops from 71× to 1× a torch at equal distance while keeping its reach. Better later: drive intensity from `lights.fade` (already stored, no schema change) with a calibration pass. No converter change, no schema bump, no reconversion.

## What is still unknown

Each gap's report carries the exact `sqlite3`/`tools/research` command; **someone with a shell must run them.** The `LGTM` polarity is arithmetic on three records and is a hard prerequisite for gap 2.
- Documentation gaps: the `Max Angle` reference frame and blend formula, and whether `dir_proj` is object- or world-space (gap 3); Skyrim's exact light attenuation curve (gap 4.3, though the fix does not depend on it); the weather-roll algorithm (gap 2, avoided by taking the first `WLST` entry).
- Not checked: whether any Blackreach model depends on a `.bgem` (gap 1); the ice base texture's actual pixels (gap 3); the exact `EMISSIVE_EXPOSURE` and all calibrated thresholds.
