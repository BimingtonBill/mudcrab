# How the demo's look differs from Skyrim: a ranked list (2026-09-24)

The user asked for a ranked list of the differences between the demo engine and Skyrim, now that
the reference set is ready (Phase 2 Dev, 2026-09-24). Each item says what is wrong, how many of the
61 usable shots show it, which code causes it, and which session should fix it.

## How it was made

- **Shots:** the 61 poses graded `matched` or `close` in `local/reference/pose-grades.json`, from
  `tools/reference/{riverwood_shots,uesp_shots_4x3,world_shots_4x3,world_shots_wide}.json`.
- **Renders:** the demo engine on `C:\Modding\SkyrimConverted` (schema 19), taken 18:23 the same
  day (`C:\Modding\wkj-tools\shots-hand\demo-*`). The upstream-engine renders (`up15-*`) were used
  only to tell fork-caused gaps from shared ones.
- **Numbers:** `tools/research/exposure_stats.py`, joined with the grades by
  `local/reference/look-gaps/join.py` (`joined.json`).
- **Eyes:** four Claude reviewers looked at every side-by-side pair
  (`local/reference/look-gaps/pairs-*/`) and tagged each one from a fixed list
  (`reviews.json`). Claude checked the surprising claims on the images themselves: Candlehearth
  Hall, Riverwood Trader, Whiterun and Morthal, plus demo against upstream for Whiterun and
  Morthal (`demo-vs-up15.jpg`).

What was measured over all 61 shots (median, Skyrim against ours):

| | Skyrim | Demo |
|---|---|---|
| Near-black pixels | 36.9 % | 4.7 % |
| Brightest 5 % starts at | 0.54 | 0.39 |
| Saturation | 0.43 | 0.35 |
| Mean brightness | 1.0 | 1.04 |

Overall brightness is about right. The spread is what is missing: Skyrim's darks are far darker
and its highlights brighter. Outdoors, Skyrim has 23 % near-black pixels and the demo 0.2 %.

## The ranked list

Rank weighs how many shots show a gap, how much a player notices it, and whether one cause
explains it. "Fork lighting" means the lighting work that lives only on the `portal` branch until
upstream issue #24 is answered (`atmosphere.rs`, `lights.rs`, per-space lighting `66ee786`, glow
`23adbb4`).

1. **Flat, hazy light everywhere** (34 shots flat, 27 wrong colour grade, 19 with wrong fog).
   Shadows are grey instead of dark, and a haze lies over outdoor scenes. Some interiors
   (Candlehearth Hall, Honeyside, the Temple of Kynareth) and Alftand are filled with a grey or
   blue fog that Skyrim does not have.
   *Cause:* fork lighting. The upstream engine renders the same Morthal and Whiterun views with
   deep cast shadows, so the ambient level and the per-space fog are what flatten the picture.
   *Measure:* near-black pixels and the p05/p95 spread above.
2. **The city worldspaces are dark by day** (Whiterun, Windhelm, Solitude). The houses and ground
   are near-black under a normal sky, lit only by windows.
   *Cause:* fork lighting. Upstream lights the same Whiterun view with sun. Most likely the
   per-space lighting gives a child worldspace no sun or an interior-style ambient; not yet traced.
3. **Fire, candle flames and light beams drawn as solid white shapes** (the four Riverwood
   interiors, Candlehearth Hall, the Palace of the Kings, High Hrothgar, Whiterun and Winterhold).
   Hearths show a white shard instead of fire, window light shafts are white bars, and candles are
   unlit. This also takes away most of the warm firelight (19 shots have the wrong light colour).
   *Cause:* probably the effect-shader meshes (Skyrim's `BSEffectShaderProperty`: flames, shafts
   and glow cards), drawn as opaque lit surfaces. Shared engine and converter; not yet traced.
4. **No sky** (18 shots). A flat colour where Skyrim has clouds and a gradient. Upstream has none
   either. Shared; already in the backlog ("No weather or overcast sky").
5. **Water white or missing** (6 shots). The Riverwood river and the Guardian Stones lake are
   white; Riften's fishery is flat white; Solitude's sea is a pale patch; Morthal's lake is absent
   in both engines. Shared; the white sheet is in the backlog, the missing lake is new.
6. **Glacier ice drawn as green blotches** (almost every Alftand exterior). Shared; already in the
   backlog ("Ice reads as rock", needs environment maps).
7. **Distant land and far objects missing** (5 shots). Shared; Phase 2's distant-LOD branches
   (`phase2/lod-*`) are the fix.
8. **Blackreach's glowing plants burn out to white** (3 shots, up to 17 % flat white). Skyrim keeps
   them a saturated blue-green. *Cause:* fork glow calibration (`EMISSIVE_EXPOSURE`).
9. **Missing nearby things** (7 shots): rugs, clutter, Morthal's boats. Recheck after the
   overnight reconversion, which adds six renderable base types
   (`phase2/renderable-base-types`); what is still missing afterwards is a real gap.

Nothing on this list is specific to the portal. The shots stand still and never look through a
doorway; the doorway's own look is checked by the tour frames, not by these shots.

## Which session fixes what

- **Portal Dev (this checkout), because the code exists only here:** items 1, 2 and 8, the fork
  lighting. The fixes go upstream later with the lighting work, if #24 is answered yes.
- **Phase 2 Dev:** items 3 to 7, shared engine and converter work that upstream needs too.
- **After the reconversion:** item 9.
